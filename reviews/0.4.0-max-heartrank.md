# 0.4.0 `max` dogfood: HeartRank

`shipreel max` run on HeartRank (a live league table of who Nigerian X loves, paid in Hearts), with the brief filled from the code as `references/campaign.md` says, then every piece read and its video frames looked at. Three full renders: 25 pieces, 5 per platform, 15 videos.

## The brief

Pre-fill gave the README's promise, the verified claims and the git story; the audience, pain, workaround, objections, CTA, angles and hashtags were written from the code (landing copy, the send flow, the no-withdrawal rule, the dollar packs). Every proof line is a verified claim or a git fact: `1 day · 13 commits · 1 contributor` is the real history (all 13 commits on 2026-08-27). No numbers were added.

## What was weak, and what changed

| Piece | Before | Fix (in the generator, so every project gets it) |
|---|---|---|
| pain-hook | "Fan wars on X are all talk?" (a statement forced into a question) | the first sentence of the pain is the hook, as written (`angles.mjs`) |
| pain-hook | caption body was the promise, same as three other pieces | the rest of the pain is the body ("Every argument about who people really love ends in screenshots and ratios, with nothing to settle it."), then the promise |
| hot-take | the wording did not read right when the workaround is a list | "Hot take: X prove nothing.", then "HeartRank settles it." |
| proof | hook "A live league table." (a label, not a claim) | leads with the first proof point of five or more words: "Money buys hearts. Hearts buy rank." |
| demo | beats repeated the proof piece | the promise, then one proof point; the screens carry it |
| build-story | ended on "The week's table is clean", out of context | "I built HeartRank in a day. 13 commits. One developer." (git facts only; no build story past 30 days) |
| all captions | proof lines lost their full stop | caption lines end as sentences; cards keep them bare |
| videos | titles in the system font | the brand's display face (Anton) fetched once, shipped in the composition |
| vertical videos | an 84-character hook cut to "...and follower…" | a vertical title wraps to 5 lines before it shrinks or cuts |
| videos | "HeartRank settles it." said twice (subtitle and the second screen) | each beat is said once: subtitle, one per screen, the rest as highlights |
| `--platforms` | re-running one platform deleted every other platform's pieces | only the platforms being made are cleared |

## After

Every TikTok and Instagram caption now has a different second line; no two pieces on a platform share a hook (`campaign_check` agrees). The claims check passes with nothing uncited. Hashtags are the brief's (`#NigerianTwitter #Afrobeats #NaijaTwitter`) plus `#HeartRank`, within each platform's range.

## Still the agent's job (by design)

- Hooks are a floor. The captions are true and distinct but plain; `/shipreel max` step 3 rewrites them in the brief's tone. Running the script alone gives a campaign you could post, not the best one.
- Vertical videos centre the content and leave the top and bottom empty, which keeps clear of TikTok's UI but is quiet. A vertical-first layout (bigger screens, title over the screen) is the next improvement to the card-video engine.
- With `--platforms`, `calendar.md` and `index.html` cover only the platforms in that run.
- Tracked links per piece (`?src=tiktok-01`) would show which angle works; not built yet.
