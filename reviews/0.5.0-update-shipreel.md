# 0.5.0 `max update` dogfood: shipreel

`shipreel update` was run on a copy of the shipreel campaign (27 pieces, built before 0.5, so it had no recorded commit and no claims copy). It was run at Day 1 (5 pieces posted, the two 0.4.1 announcements among them), with the note "0.4.2: slide headlines take a third or fourth line instead of being cut. Every page has the shipreel favicon." Each run started from a fresh copy. The posted pieces were hashed before and after.

## What was weak, and what changed

| Run | Before | Fix (in the update, so every campaign gets it) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The 0.4.2 point was "backed" by unrelated commits that shared only "line" and "instead"; the real fix (`81fcd9c`) was not first | A point must share 2 topic words, or 40% of a long point's words (`sameTopic` in `talking_points.mjs`); evidence is ranked by overlap |
| 1 | "shipreel" + "page" tied four demo pieces to the favicon point, so they were marked for rewrite | The product's own name is left out of topic matching |
| 1 | The 5-piece cap went entirely to the first point (5 platforms); the favicon point got nothing | New pieces are shared across the changes, X and LinkedIn first (news reads best as text) |
| 1 | A shared version string outranked the commit that made the change | The version bonus is +1, so word overlap decides |
| 2 | The copied campaign could not see the project's `claims.json`, so every "3 to 5 pieces" looked uncited | A campaign outside the project reads brief and claims from the project's `shipreel-output` (`outDirFor`), or from `--out` |
| 2 | Two update pieces on one platform failed the check as "same angle" | Update pieces may share the angle; only their hooks are compared |
| 3 | Two polished captions were thrown away because the claim "a two-week calendar" cited a quote (`…and an upload page`) that 0.4.1 removed from the README | A sourced claim holds while its quote **or its own words** are in the file. A moved quote is listed in `changes.md` under "claims.json quotes to update" and nothing is rewritten. (shipreel's own `claims.json` now quotes "a two-week calendar and a posting desk".) |
| fixture | A one-commit project's build story said "1 commits." | "One commit." (`angles.mjs`) |
| fixture | Pieces quoting the git story ("1 day · 1 commit") went stale as the repo grew, and the rewrite repeated the old line because brief.md still had it | Unsourced facts are stale when today's dossier no longer states them; `freshBrief` redrafts from today's line; `changes.md` lists the stale brief.md lines (brief.md is the user's and is not edited) |

## After

- Both points are backed, with the right evidence first: `81fcd9c` and `f055b0d` for the headlines, `a7ff320` for the favicon.
- 4 pieces were added (`x/06-update`, `linkedin/06-update`, `x/07-update`, `linkedin/07-update`), and they lead Day 2. Nothing was rewritten.
- The 5 posted pieces are byte-identical, and Day 1 still lists exactly them.
- The campaign check passes. Posted pieces are skipped by the check, since they are history, but their hooks still count against repeats.

## Still the agent's job (by design)

- The update pieces' copy is a floor: the hook is the user's sentence ("0.4.2: slide headlines take…"), then the promise and the call to action. Step 6 of the update flow rewrites them in the brief's tone, as `max` does.
- A backed piece of feedback ("people keep asking if it works for Python") is listed with its evidence and answered by the agent, never turned into a quote from a person.
- A campaign built before 0.5 has no recorded commit, so the first update reads the whole history. The matching thresholds keep that from producing false evidence, but the second update onward is tighter.
- The desk only writes `posted.json` directly in browsers with folder access (Chrome and Edge). Elsewhere it downloads the file and asks you to move it into the campaign folder.
